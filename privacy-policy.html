Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

The short version

  • Big Blast has no accounts, no sign-in, and we never collect your name, email, or other personal details ourselves.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which involves some automatic data collection and network activity — see "Advertising" below.
  • Your game progress (scores, stats, settings, unlocks) is saved only on your own device.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it saved locally.

This policy explains how Big Blast ("the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the Big Blast mobile application published by Bezaz Games.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts.

The app does include advertising software (Google AdMob) — see "Advertising" below for what that involves. Outside of ads, there is no analytics or tracking software in the app, and we do not otherwise transmit data to ourselves or anyone else.

Advertising

Big Blast shows ads through Google AdMob, Google's mobile advertising platform. To select and deliver ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. This happens on Google's own systems — we never receive or have access to this data ourselves.

This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Depending on your device and region, you may be able to limit ad personalization through your device's own privacy settings (for example, Android's advertising ID controls).

Information stored on your device

So that your progress is still there when you come back, the app saves game data locally on your device using standard app storage. This includes:

  • Your high score, rank, and score history
  • Lifetime statistics such as games played and lines cleared
  • In-game currency, owned items, and unlocked themes
  • Daily and weekly challenge progress and login streak
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, and accessibility preferences

This information never leaves your device. We have no access to it, no copy of it, and no ability to retrieve it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information, and we have none of our own to share, since we never collect any directly. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service, which may share the automatically-collected data described in "Advertising" above with Google's advertising and measurement partners as part of delivering and measuring ads. Beyond that, there are no analytics providers, data brokers, or other third-party services integrated into the app.

Children's privacy

Big Blast is not directed specifically at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — there is no mechanism in the app by which we could collect or transmit it. Because the app shows ads, parents should be aware that Google AdMob may still collect the automatically-generated advertising data described above; this is Google's data handling, not ours. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, contact us and we will address it.

Deleting your data

Because all data is stored on your device, you control it completely. You can remove it by:

  • Uninstalling the app, which deletes all saved data, or
  • Clearing the app's storage through your device settings, or
  • Using the in-app reset options in the Settings menu

Resetting or uninstalling cannot be undone, and progress cannot be recovered afterwards, since no backup exists outside your device. This does not affect ad-related data already processed by Google, which follows Google's own retention practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy already reflects the app's current use of Google AdMob for advertising. If anything else changes in a way that affects this policy — for example, if real in-app purchases or additional online features are added in a future version — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before the relevant version is released.
