The short version

This policy explains how Big Blast ("the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the Big Blast mobile application published by [DEVELOPER NAME].

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts. It does not request any device permissions.

The app does not contain analytics, tracking, or advertising software, and does not transmit data to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

So that your progress is still there when you come back, the app saves game data locally on your device using standard app storage. This includes:

  • Your high score, rank, and score history
  • Lifetime statistics such as games played and lines cleared
  • In-game currency, owned items, and unlocked themes
  • Daily and weekly challenge progress and login streak
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, and accessibility preferences

This information never leaves your device. We have no access to it, no copy of it, and no ability to retrieve it.

Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information, because we do not collect any. There are no third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics providers integrated into the app.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and there is no mechanism in the app by which such data could be collected or transmitted.

Deleting your data

Because all data is stored on your device, you control it completely. You can remove it by:

  • Uninstalling the app, which deletes all saved data, or
  • Clearing the app's storage through your device settings, or
  • Using the in-app reset options in the Settings menu

Resetting or uninstalling cannot be undone, and progress cannot be recovered afterwards, since no backup exists outside your device.

Changes to this policy
